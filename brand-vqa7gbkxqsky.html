CapacityDigital
  • Same mark, never redrawn. Neon is the agency's colour for it; blue belongs to the platform. Never swap them.
  • The lockup is the mark, CAPACITY in the display face, and DIGITAL small in mono. Same family as the platform, clearly the sibling.
  • Clearspace: one arm's width, all sides. Never rotated, outlined or shadowed. Stickers tilt, the mark doesn't.
Neon#ff2e88
Blush#ff9cc6
Night#0a0a0a
Ash#161616
Paper#f6f6f6
Muted#8f8f8f
  • Night is the default. Paper is for proof: case studies, reports, proposals.
  • Neon is the moment: the sold out, the live number, the button. One full Neon field per set of posts, no more.
  • Text on Neon is always Night, never white. Blush is Neon's voice on dark, for small type. On paper, type in Neon Deep #e0106c.
  • The liquid gradients retire. No purple, no orange (that's E Labs), no teal (that's Furiosa).

Don't
Elevating venue experiences through innovative, data-driven creative solutions.
  • Lead with the work. Say what it is in one line, then show it.
  • No gags, no invented stories, no quotes nobody said. If there's no number yet, leave the bracket in.
  • Numbers only when they're good and real. One per piece, never a wall of stats.
  • "We" is fine: the agency is people. Mention the platform where it genuinely helps the work, the way Soma mentions Fatsoma. Never as a colour block.
  • No slogans that could belong to anyone, never "guaranteed". No em dashes.
