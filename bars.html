Questions,
answered.

Can guests book a table on our own site?

Yes. Bookings run under your own name on your own site, from the booking page to the confirmation email, and every booking lands on the guest’s profile. Connections to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite are coming soon, for the ticketed nights you sell there.

Does it match what people spend at the bar to them?

No. The till gives you takings by hour and your best sellers for the whole bar, night by night. What each guest booked and how often they come sits on their profile. Square, EPOS Now and ICRTouch connections are coming soon.

What about guest WiFi sign-ups?

They become profiles like everything else, with consent tracked per channel, so someone who joined the WiFi on Friday is someone you can legally invite back on Tuesday.

Does this work for one bar or a group?

Both. A single bar runs on one screen. Groups run every site from the same one, with the numbers kept separate and added up.

We have no one to run marketing. Does that matter?

No. The lists, the messages and the Monday report are built to be run by whoever runs the bar. Or hand it over: our team works inside the same platform.
