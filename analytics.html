Every visit, followed
to the sale.

Page views tell you people turned up. Because your tickets and bookings sell on your own site, Capacity follows each visit through to the payment and puts a number on it.

01

Counted on your own site

Sessions, visitors and pages, tracked on the site where your tickets and bookings sell, so nothing goes missing between the page and the payment.

02

Where they came from

Instagram, Google, an email or typed straight in. Every session carries its source, including the links in your own posts and sends.

03

Which event pages sell

Views, bookings and revenue for each event page side by side. A page with plenty of visits and few bookings shows up straight away.

04

The journey, step by step

From the first visit to the event page, the checkout and the booking. See the step where people stop, and fix that one first.

05

Revenue by source, in pounds

Not clicks or likes. What each channel actually took through the checkout, so you know which post was worth the time.

06

Joined to the customer

When a visitor books, the visit lands on their profile, so the marketing that follows knows what brought them in the first time.
