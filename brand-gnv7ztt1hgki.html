  • Black on light, white on dark. Blue when it stands alone. The gradient is for big art moments.
  • Clearspace: one arm's width, all sides. 20px minimum on screen.
  • Never rotated, outlined, shadowed or redrawn.

  • Rethink Sans, weights 400 to 800. Nothing lighter.

Do: one recognisable object
Don't: a diagram of the idea
  • One object per feature or venue type: a ticket, a megaphone, the CDJs, a festival tent. Something you would recognise across a room.
  • Built from points (points.js), lit from the top left, in brand blue on dark. Every point drifts along its surface; nothing sits frozen.
  • Round objects turn; flat ones sway about their best side and are never seen edge on. Everything can be dragged.
  • The mark appears as an object in one place only: at the centre of the integrations hub.
Do: coded, live, real numbers

Don't: a screenshot of a screen
  • Product moments are built in HTML from the app's own tokens (the .ui kit in site.css), never exported as pictures. They stay sharp, they can move, they never go stale.
  • Every number is plausible for one real venue, and the live ones tick. Plain English labels: "Through the door", not "Footfall".
  • Pair a product card with its object: the object says what it is, the card proves it works.
