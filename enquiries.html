Every way they get in touch,
answered from one place.

A booth enquiry on Instagram, a birthday through the website, a question on WhatsApp at midnight. They all arrive in the same inbox, next to what that person has already booked and spent with you.

01

Every channel lands here

Website forms, email, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook messages arrive in one inbox, newest first. No more checking five apps before you open.

02

One thread per customer

Someone emails on Tuesday and follows up on Instagram on Thursday. It is still one conversation, with one person, in one place.

03

Their history beside the message

How often they come, what they spend and what they booked last time, right next to what they just asked. You know who you are talking to before you reply.

04

A drafted reply, ready to check

Every enquiry comes with a reply already written from what they asked and what they have booked before. Read it, change what you like, send it.

05

Nothing slips through

Anything without a reply stays marked and counted until somebody answers it, so a booth for eight does not sit unread over the weekend.

06

Answered in the channel they used

You reply from the inbox and it goes back out on WhatsApp, Instagram or email, wherever they asked. The whole exchange stays on their profile.
