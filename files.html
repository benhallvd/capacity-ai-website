One place for everything
the week runs on.

Every night runs on a pile of files: flyers, stories, set times, menus, contracts. Kept in one place and filed by event, they are there when a post, an email or a promoter needs them.

01

Everything in one library

Artwork, video, menus, contracts and logos. Upload from a laptop or a phone, and the whole team works from the same files.

02

A folder for every event

The flyer, the stories, the set times and the DJ’s contract for Saturday sit together, under Saturday.

03

Share by link, on your terms

Send a promoter a link to the folder they need. Choose view only or let them upload, and switch the link off whenever you like.

04

Use it where you need it

Pick artwork from here for a post, an email or an event page. No downloading it to upload it again somewhere else.

05

Every version kept

Upload a new flyer over the old one and the earlier versions stay, so you can see what changed and go back.

06

Staff see what they need

The bar team gets the menus, promoters get their own events, and contracts stay with the people who sign them.

Drop it in once.
Use it everywhere.

Files land in the right event folder as they upload. Pick the new flyer and the scheduled post and the event page both take it, and the promoter gets a link that can look but not touch.
