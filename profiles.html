One record for
every guest.

Your guests leave a trail across five different systems, each with its own half of the story. Capacity puts the halves together, so you can see who someone is, how often they come, and whether you are allowed to tell them about Saturday.

01

Every source, one person

Ticket sales, table bookings, WiFi sign-ups, enquiries and socials all land on the same profile.

02

The same guest, never twice

When someone turns up somewhere new, they are matched to the profile they already have on email or phone, not added as a second row.

03

Tagged by what they do

Regular, High spend, Booth guest, Lapsed. Tags follow what each person books and spends, so they stay right without anyone keeping them.

04

Consent on every channel

Email, SMS and WhatsApp consent recorded separately, with where and when it was given. GDPR-safe from the first sign-up.

05

Segments in plain terms

“Came three or more times, not since August.” Build a list from visits, spend, rooms and dates, and see how many you can reach.

06

Ready to reach

Send to a segment by email, SMS or WhatsApp from the same screen you built it on. No exporting, no second tool.
