Community and marketing,
in one place.

Your regulars already talk on WhatsApp. Run the community there, send from the same screen your ticket sales and door numbers are on, and see what every message actually did.

01

Communities and broadcast lists

Keep Labyrinth Insiders, the Rooftop regulars and your booth customers as separate lists, each built from people who said yes to hearing from you here.

02

Guestlists by reply

Post “Reply IN and you’re on it” and every IN becomes a name on the guestlist, plus-ones counted, with no one copying names across by hand.

03

Sent from the screen you measure on

Write it, pick the list, send it now or set a time. The message sits next to the sales and door numbers it is meant to move.

04

Read rates and replies, in plain numbers

How many people opened it, how many answered, and how many of them went on to buy a ticket or join the list.

05

Consent tracked per channel

A yes to WhatsApp is recorded on the guest’s profile, separately from email and SMS, with where they gave it. Every send stays GDPR-safe.

06

Replies land in your inbox

Anything that is not an IN, a question about doors or a table for six, arrives in Enquiries with the guest’s history beside it.
