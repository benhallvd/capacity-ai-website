The whole bar,
read properly.

Your till counts the money. Capacity tells you where it came from: which bar, which hour and which drink. All of it beside the door count and the tickets you already run here.

01

Every till, one screen

Takings from every bar land in Capacity as they ring through, so you see the whole venue at once rather than one terminal at a time.

02

Bar by bar, hour by hour

Split the night by bar and by hour. See when the Rooftop peaks, when Barcadia goes quiet, and where the staff need to be next Saturday.

03

Best sellers, ranked

What sold, how many and for how much, per bar and per night. The drink that is climbing and the one nobody orders both show up.

04

Spend per head, worked out

Till takings divided by the people the door scanned in. One number that tells you whether a busy night was also a good one.

05

Compared, not just counted

Every night sits next to last week and the same night last year, so you can tell a good Saturday from a normal one at a glance.

06

Stock you can plan

What sold last Saturday, bar by bar and hour by hour, tells you what to have in and where before the next one.
