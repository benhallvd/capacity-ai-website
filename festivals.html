Questions,
answered.

Can we keep selling where we sell now?

Yes. Connections to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite are coming soon, so those buyers will land in the same list. Run both, or move the on-sale to your own site when it suits you and the buyer is yours from the first ticket.

Does it handle tiers and phases?

Yes. Ticket types, allocations, presales and release windows, with the prices you set and the next tier opening when the last one sells.

What about everyone who came before we joined?

Bring them with you. Our team connects your accounts and brings your existing customer data across when we set you up, so last year’s crowd is there before the next announcement.

We sell once a year. How is it priced?

A fixed monthly subscription per organiser, so you know the number before you start. We will walk you through exact pricing on the demo once we understand your setup.

Is our customer data safe?

Yes. Capture and messaging are GDPR-compliant end to end, with consent recorded per channel. Your data stays yours, we will never sell it on, and you can export it whenever you like.

Bring last year’s on-sale. We will show you where each tier sold, who came back, and who you could reach before the next announcement.
