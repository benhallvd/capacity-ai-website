A long gap between editions

After the weekend, ticket buyers tend not to hear much until the next announcement, and other events are in touch with them in the meantime.

Buyers sit on the ticket site’s list

When tickets sell through a marketplace, most of the buyer’s details stay with it, and what comes back to you is a sales total and a payout.

What we set up and run.

Our team sets it up around your ticketing and runs it through to the next edition.

01

Each tier, set against last year

Each release is tracked day by day against the same point last year, so a slower tier shows up months before the event.

02

In touch between editions

Ticket buyers, ticket scans and sign-ups become profiles sorted by the years they came, and our team keeps in touch with them between editions by email or on WhatsApp.

03

Ticketing under your own name

If you want to sell direct, we set up tiers and phases on your own site with your name on every screen, up to the wallet pass. If you sell through DICE or Skiddle, we connect to both.

Questions,
answered.

Can we keep selling where we sell now?

Yes. We connect to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite, so those buyers land on the same list. If you later want to sell on your own site as well, we can set that up, and nothing has to be switched off.

Does it handle tiers and phases?

Yes. Ticket types, allocations, presales and release windows, with the prices you set and the next tier opening when the last one sells.

What about everyone who came in previous years?

They come with you. Our team connects your accounts and moves your existing customer data across during setup, so last year’s crowd is there before the next announcement.

We sell once a year. How is it priced?

Every business is different, so we don’t publish prices. We’ll talk it through once we know how yours runs.

Is our customer data safe?

Yes. Capture and messaging are GDPR compliant, with consent recorded per channel. Your data stays yours, we will never sell it on, and you can export it whenever you like.
