The answer, and what
to do about it.

Getting a straight answer out of venue data usually means an export, a spreadsheet and an evening. Here you type the question the way you would ask your manager, and the answer comes back with the next step on it.

01

Ask in plain English

No filters to set and no report to build. Type the question the way you would say it, and get the number and the why.

02

Answers from your live data

Tickets, bookings, the till, enquiries and messaging, all in the same system. It answers from your venue, not from a generic model.

03

The takeaway attached

Every answer comes with the figure, a small chart or list to show it, and one line on what it means for the week ahead.

04

Flags things before you ask

A night slipping, regulars who have gone quiet, a ticket tier about to sell out. Raised while there is still time to act.

05

From answer to action

Open the list behind the number, or message those guests from the same screen. No exporting to another tool.

06

Your data stays yours

It reads your venue’s data to answer you. That data stays yours, GDPR-compliant end to end, and is never sold on.
