Written up for Monday

September took £84,240, 12% more than August. Saturday Residency did half of it on its own: £41,600 across four Saturdays.

Thursday grew fastest. It took £9,800, up 34% on August, and most of that growth was people who had never been in before.

1,902 new guests came through the door, and 58% of the crowd had been before. 412 regulars who came three times or more before August have not been back.

What worked. The Friday guestlist on WhatsApp drew 312 replies, and Instagram sold more tickets than any other channel: 1,870 of them.

Next. Ask the 412 back for Saturday Residency on 3 October, and open a second room on Thursdays while the night is growing.
