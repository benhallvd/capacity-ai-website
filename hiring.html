Marketing Manager

Full timeRemote, UK

You’ll manage marketing delivery for our clients, taking each campaign from the plan through to the results. You’ll work with the team making the creative and with the venues and promoters it’s for, keeping the work on brief and on time.

Starting January 2027

About this role

You’ll manage the marketing we deliver for our clients, working across venues and promoters with different events and audiences. Each client needs a clear plan, work that goes out on brief and on time, and an honest view of how it performed.

Starting January 2027

Key responsibilities

  • Plan marketing around each client’s events and trading calendar
  • Run paid social, email and SMS campaigns from brief to launch
  • Brief the creative team and check the work before it goes out
  • Report on results and use them to shape what runs next
  • Keep deadlines clear across several clients at once

Who we’re looking for

  • Experience in marketing delivery, ideally in hospitality, events or nightlife.
  • Hands-on knowledge of Meta ads, email and campaign reporting
  • Able to keep several clients moving at once
  • Clear with clients and with the team
  • Based in the UK. This is a remote role, open to UK applicants only.

Account Manager

Full timeRemote, UK

You’ll manage our client relationships, as the first person each venue and promoter talks to. You’ll onboard new clients, train their teams to use Capacity and stay close as the work with them grows.

Starting January 2027

About this role

You’ll be the main contact for our clients from the day they join. You’ll get to know how each business runs, make sure their team is confident using Capacity, and keep the work pointed at what matters most to them.

Starting January 2027

Key responsibilities

  • Manage a group of client relationships as their main contact
  • Onboard new clients and get them set up around how they run
  • Train client teams to use Capacity
  • Hold regular check-ins and walk clients through how the work is performing
  • Bring client feedback back to the team and follow it through

Who we’re looking for

  • Experience in account management or client success, ideally in hospitality, events or nightlife.
  • Confident training people and explaining things plainly
  • Good at building trust with owners and operators
  • Able to keep several clients moving at once
  • Based in the UK. This is a remote role, open to UK applicants only.
