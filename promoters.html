Questions,
answered.

Which ticket sites can you report from?

The ones promoters usually sell on. We connect to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite, and each night’s sales and buyers come through as they happen.

Can the reports go straight to the venue?

Yes. Each night or month is written up in plain English, and you can send it to the venue as it stands or export it as a PDF, with the figures underneath as a spreadsheet.

We run a weekly night and one-offs. Is that one account?

Yes. Every night sits in the same place, the weekly one, the one-offs and any away dates, with one list across all of them. Reports cover a single night or a whole month, with the numbers side by side and combined.

Can we keep selling where we sell now?

Yes. Nothing changes at the checkout or on your event pages. We connect to the ticketing you already use and build the reports and your list on top of it, so your buyers keep buying the way they do today.

Is the customer data ours?

Yes. Capture and messaging are GDPR compliant, with consent recorded per channel. Your data stays yours, we will never sell it on, and you can export it whenever you like.
