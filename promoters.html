Questions,
answered.

Can we keep selling where we sell now?

Yes. Connections to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite are coming soon, so those buyers will land on your list too. Sell on your own site when it suits you, and the buyer is yours from the first ticket.

How does rep commission work?

Each rep gets access to the nights they are working and nothing else. You set their rate, and their sales and commission add up as they sell, so the totals are there the morning after rather than at the end of a spreadsheet.

We run a weekly night and one-offs. Is that one account?

Yes. Every night sits in the same place, the weekly one, the one-offs and any away dates, with one list across all of them. Report on a single night or a whole month, with the numbers side by side and combined.

What about the list we already have?

Bring it with you. Our team connects your accounts and brings your existing customer data across when we set you up, and anyone who buys from then on is matched to the profile they already have.

Is the customer data ours?

Yes. Capture and messaging are GDPR-compliant end to end, with consent recorded per channel. Your data stays yours, we will never sell it on, and you can export it whenever you like.

See it on your own numbers.

Bring your last few months of nights. We will show you how many buyers came to more than one, and how many you could reach before the next.
