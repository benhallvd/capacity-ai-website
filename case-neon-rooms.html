Every week started
from zero.

The team could see how many tickets had sold and nothing about who had bought them. Every message went to the whole list, the same people got asked twice, and the regulars got nothing.

The door had the data. The till had the data. The ticketing platform had the data. None of it was in the same place, and none of it had a name attached.

What changed

01

Tickets on their own site

Ticketing moved onto their own website under their own name, so every buyer landed in their list rather than a marketplace’s.

02

The regulars, found

Two years of tickets, door scans and WiFi sign-ups became 4,100 profiles. A quarter had been more than once, and nobody knew.

03

Messages that knew who they were for

Regulars hear first on WhatsApp, guests who drifted get a note of their own, and first-timers get a reason to come back.

We were guessing every week. Now the first thing I look at on a Monday is who came, who came back, and who has not been since February.
