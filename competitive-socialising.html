Questions,
answered.

Can people book on our own site?

Yes. Bookings run under your own name on your own site, from the booking page to the confirmation email, and every booker lands on a profile. If you sell some sessions through DICE, Skiddle, FIXR or Eventbrite, those connect too.

Can we see which slots sit empty?

Yes. Every booking lands in one place, so the dashboard shows today by slot and where there is room. The Monday report sums up the week, and Capacity Intelligence picks out which slots stay quiet and which bookers only ever come at the weekend, so you know who to offer them to.

Can we sell parties and packages with food and drink?

Yes. They are set up as their own booking types, with their own prices and allocations, next to the standard slots. Parties that need a conversation first come into the enquiries inbox.

Does the till link what a booking spends to the booker?

No. The till gives you food and drink takings by bar and by hour, and your best sellers, for the whole venue. Bookings and visits sit on each booker’s profile. Square, EPOS Now and ICRTouch all connect.

Does this work across several sites?

Yes. Each site has its own view, and if you run several, reports cover each site and the whole group.
