Questions,
answered.

Can people book on our own site?

Yes. Bookings run under your own name on your own site, from the booking page to the confirmation email, and every booker lands on a profile. If you sell through DICE, Skiddle, FIXR or Eventbrite today, connections to those are coming soon.

Can we see which slots sit empty?

Yes. Every booking lands in one place, so the dashboard shows today by slot and where the gaps are. The Monday report sums up the week, and Intelligence tells you which slots stay quiet and which bookers only ever come at the weekend, so you know who to offer them to.

Can we sell parties and packages with food and drink?

Yes. Set them up as their own booking types, with their own prices and allocations, next to the standard slots. Parties that need a conversation first come into the enquiries inbox.

Does the till link what a booking spends to the booker?

No. The till gives you food and drink takings by bar and by hour, and your best sellers, for the whole venue. Bookings and visits sit on each profile. Square, EPOS Now and ICRTouch connections are coming soon.

Does this work across several sites?

Yes. A single venue runs on one screen. If you run several sites, they all run from the same one, with the numbers kept separate and added up.
