Outreach.

  • Gifts for one owner at a time. The headline says what Capacity is. The audit is the small action, on the ticket.
  • Print is flat: the tiles straight on, in coarse cells. The turned 3D render stays on screens.
  • The box is quiet: matte black with a small engraved mark, and the colour saved for inside.
  • The light and grain carry print when the mark is on them: liners, straps, banners.
  • A box made for someone carries their logo. Capacity keeps its type and mark.
  • Dashed text is a placeholder. Replace it before anything goes out.
