Every sign-up lands
in the same list.

People will give you their details on the night, on your site and from your socials, if the form is in front of them. Put one everywhere they are, and let every answer go straight to the profile it belongs to.

01

On your own site

Embed a form on any page of your website, in your own colours, or link to a hosted page on your domain.

02

In your bio

A link-in-bio page with the form on it for Instagram and TikTok, so a follower becomes a contact.

03

At the door and on the tables

A QR code for the door, the tables or the bar. Guests scan it, sign up on their phone, and you know which table it came from.

04

On the WiFi

Guest WiFi asks for an email before it connects, and that sign-up lands with everything else.

05

Consent, channel by channel

Separate ticks for email, SMS and WhatsApp. The exact wording they agreed to is saved with the time and the form.

06

Straight into a profile

Every sign-up becomes a customer profile. Anyone you already know is matched to the record they have, not added twice.

One form.
Everywhere they are.

The same form on your site, behind the link in your bio and on a card on the table. Wherever someone fills it in, they arrive in one list, with where they came from and what they agreed to.
