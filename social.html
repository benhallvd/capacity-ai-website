Plan it once.
Post it everywhere.

Social is where most of your tickets start. Planning the week, getting it signed off, posting it and knowing what it sold all sit in one place, next to the tickets themselves.

01

One calendar for every channel

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook on one week view. Plan a whole week in one sitting and schedule it all in one pass.

02

One caption, fitted to each platform

Write it once and get a version for each platform. Change any of them before it goes out.

03

Drafts and approvals

Anyone on the team can draft a post. Nothing goes out until someone with sign-off has approved it.

04

Posted when people are looking

Best times come from when your own followers are on, so the Friday post goes out when they are actually scrolling.

05

See which posts sold

Every ticket bought from a post is tracked back to it. Tickets and profile visits for each post, not just likes.

06

Your own reach, joined up

Organic posts on your own accounts, no paid ads. Artwork comes straight from Files, and every buyer lands on a customer profile.

See which posts
actually sold.

When someone buys from a post, the ticket lands on that post. So the week ranks itself by what it sold, not by what it was liked.
