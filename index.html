Anything else, ask us during the audit.

What does Capacity do?

We build technology into your business, and our team does the work. Depending on what will make the most difference to you, that might start with ticketing on your own site or a clearer view of who your customers are, and we take on more as we learn how you run.

Do we have to change the tools we use?

No. We connect to the systems you already run, from your till to your email tool, so nothing gets switched off and your team keeps working the way it does now.

Who does the work?

Our team does. You keep sign-off on anything that goes out and can see what we are doing at any time. Most of the time we need an hour or two a week from you.

Can we keep selling where we already sell?

Yes. We connect to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite, so buyers there land in the same place as everyone else. When you are ready, your next event can sell through your own site instead.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we don’t publish prices. We’ll talk it through once we know how yours runs, and pricing is set after the audit.

What happens in the audit?

We look at how you trade today and where your customer data sits, then agree where to start and what it will involve. It is a conversation, with no commitment.

Is our customer data safe?

Yes. Your data stays yours and is never sold on. Data capture and messaging are GDPR compliant, and you can export everything whenever you like.
