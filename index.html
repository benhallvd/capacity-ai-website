Some venues are always full. Some can hardly get anyone through the door. The difference is knowing exactly who fills the room.

Capacity is the operating system for venues. It connects everything you already run, from the ticket to the till, so you can see who your customers are, reach them properly, and keep them coming back.

Enquiries

Every enquiry, one inbox

Website forms, email, WhatsApp and socials land in one place, with each customer’s history beside the message. Reply in minutes, and no booking slips through.

  • One thread per customer, whatever channel they used
  • Their bookings, spend and history sit beside the message
  • A drafted reply ready for you to check and send

Customer profiles

Universal customer profiles

Ticket buyers, table bookings, door scans, WiFi sign-ups and socials merge into one profile per person. Tagged automatically, GDPR-safe, and ready to reach.

  • Tickets, bookings, door scans and WiFi merged into one record
  • Tagged automatically by what they book and spend
  • Consent tracked per channel, GDPR-safe end to end

Ticketing

Ticketing and bookings on your own site, fully white-labelled. Your brand on every screen the customer sees, the list stays yours, and every sale lands in the same place as everything else.

  • Booking heatmaps and year-on-year comparisons
  • Quiet weeks flagged while there is still time to act

What exactly is Capacity?

The operating system for venues. Capacity brings your ticketing, bookings, enquiries, messaging, socials and sales into one platform, so you know who your customers are, can reach them properly, and can see exactly what is bringing them in.

Do I need a marketing team to use it?

No. That’s the point. Capacity gives a venue the output of a full team without hiring one. We handle the admin and the analysis; you make the calls. Multi-site groups run every venue from one screen.

Can we keep selling where we already sell?

Yes. Capacity gives you your own ticketing and booking under your own brand, and connections to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite are coming soon, so buyers there will land in the same place. Run both, or move across when it suits you.

How does setup work?

We do it with you. Our team connects your accounts, brings your existing customer data across and shapes the platform around how your venue actually works. Nothing to install, and you deal with the same people from the first call onwards.

How much does it cost?

Plans scale with your estate. We bill a fixed monthly subscription per site or organiser, so you know the number before you start. We’ll walk you through exact pricing on the demo once we understand your setup. No hard sell, no lock-in surprises.

Is our customer data safe?

Yes. Data capture and messaging are GDPR-compliant end to end, your data stays yours, and we will never sell it on. Export it whenever you like.
