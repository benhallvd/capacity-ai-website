Questions,
answered.

Do we have to move off our current ticketing?

No. We connect to DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite, so profiles build from your existing sales and nothing changes at the door. If you would rather sell on your own site with your name on every screen, we can set that up too.

Does this work for one venue or a group?

Both. For a single club, everything sits on one screen. For a group, we report site by site and across the whole group.

What about guest WiFi sign-ups?

They become profiles like everything else, with consent tracked per channel, so a sign-up at the door is someone you can legally reach on Tuesday.
