Questions,
answered.

Do we have to move off our current ticketing?

No. DICE, Skiddle, FIXR and Eventbrite all connect, so profiles build from your existing sales and nothing changes at the door. If you would rather sell on your own site with your name on every screen, that can be set up too.

Does this work for one venue or a group?

Both. For a single club, everything sits on one screen. For a group, reports cover each site and the group as a whole.

What about guest WiFi sign-ups?

They become profiles like everything else, with consent tracked per channel, so a sign-up at the door is someone you can legally reach on Tuesday.
