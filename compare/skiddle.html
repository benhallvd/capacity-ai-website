Which one.

Skiddle if

  • You want a free listing with reach.
  • The fee is the customer’s, not yours.
  • You run one-offs.

Capacity if

  • You want your own name on the ticket.
  • You want the people who came in one CRM.
  • You want it built around how you sell.

Questions.

Can we keep selling on Skiddle?

Yes. Skiddle connects, so buyers there land in the same CRM, and your next event can sell through your own site too.

What does Skiddle charge?

Its published default is 10% of the ticket price plus 27p, minimum £1, paid by the customer on top. You can pay it yourself as a commission instead. Check Skiddle’s help centre for the current rate.

What does Capacity charge?

Pricing is agreed in the audit, around how your business runs.
