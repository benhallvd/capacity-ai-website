Capacity brand.

One brand across the site, the product and everything made for them. Written for people here, and for AI in tools/brand.

Mark.

  • The logomark does the work. No wordmark in product mockups.
  • White on dark, black on light, white on blue. Blue on dark in the site nav only.
  • Clear space is the height of one arm of the mark, all the way round.

Type.

  • Mona Sans Wide for headings. Rethink Sans for everything you read.
  • Capitals for every hero and closing line on the site.
  • Sentence case for every section heading. No exceptions by length.
  • Mona never goes inside a mockup. The product stays in Rethink Sans.
  • Geist Mono for labels and readouts, in plain English.

Icons.

  • Every icon is cut from the mark: solid parts, 3 units apart, on a 48 unit grid.
  • Every diagonal is the mark’s own angle, 3 up and 2 across.
  • Two colours only, ink and one lit part. White and navy on blue.
  • This set is for the site and for assets. Mockups keep the product’s own icons.

The light.

  • The room's light as colour: pale at the top, violet and deep blue underneath, cyan on the far side. Always with grain.
  • Its best use is the mark as a window onto a real night, with the shot in black and white and the light falling on it.
  • Keep it on the mark and on photos. A bare gradient panel reads as any tech company.
  • Anyone in focus is a real person from a real night. Generated images stay as rooms and crowds, never faces.

Tiles.

  • The room as Capacity reads it: a grid of small tiles, each lit by the picture under it.
  • On photos they are a faint texture, 12 to 16%, never enough to hide a person. Nothing blurs a photo.
  • Full strength is for the mark and icons only, one bright subject on black.

Photography.

  • A great night seen from inside the room, full of people and movement.
  • Anyone in focus is a real person. Generated images are rooms and crowds only: hands, silhouettes, backs of heads.
  • Moments, not poses. Nobody lined up for the camera, nobody looking the worse for wear.
  • Black and white and sharp on the site.

Prompts for rooms and crowds: midjourney-prompts.md

Graphics.

  • Pieces of the real product, cropped tight. One piece, one action.
  • Dark UI on a dotted panel, or on tiles. Real figures from the demo venue, Labyrinth.
  • No illustration, no icons in circles, no arrows between boxes, no outlined boxes as decoration.

Motion.

  • Slow and certain. Headings rise line by line. Numbers count up once.
  • Light to dark is a wipe or a timed fade, never scrubbed by scroll, so nobody parks on grey.
  • Tiles barely move until touched. The camera never moves.

Site

Ease cubic-bezier(.22, .61, .36, 1). Entrances run .6 to .9s.

Video

Remotion, in tools/remotion. Headline, Stat, Fragment, Feature, Stack and Close, each using these tokens, the icons and the tile field. Outline type is for video only, in Stack.

For AI.

  • Anything that makes an asset starts from tools/brand/BRAND.md.
  • Assets are made in tools/, which never deploys. Making one never edits the site.
  • A brand change that should reach the site is a separate job, done on purpose.
tools/brand/BRAND.md

The rules: art direction, type, colour, the light, tiles, photography, graphics, motion, copy.

tools/brand/tokens.json

Colours, type, the light and motion as data, generated from the site’s tokens.css.

assets/icons

The icon set as SVGs. tools/brand/icons.json holds the same paths as data, generated from them.

tools/remotion

Video and animated posts built from those tokens.

tools/export

Exports the graphics here, and the templates and outreach pieces on their own pages.
